Workshop brand

OpenMBEE workshop brand guidelines

How to use the OpenMBEE Workshop 2027 badges, one for INCOSE IW and one for Caltech, on the web, slides and printed material. It follows the OpenMBEE brand guidelines: same palette and typeface.

Workshop badges

The badge shows a document linked to a set of model blocks: engineering information generated from, and connected to, the model. The lettering follows the logo wordmark, with OPEN in a light weight and MBEE in bold. The two badges are identical except for the venue line. Each comes in a version for dark backgrounds and one for light backgrounds.

OpenMBEE 2027 Workshop at INCOSE IW

OpenMBEE 2027 Workshop at Caltech

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin of at least x around the badge, where x is one eighth of the badge width. Keep other logos, text and page edges outside it.

x = 1/8 of the badge width

  • Minimum badge size: 96 px on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that the venue line stops being legible; use the OpenMBEE icon or plain text instead.
  • Always scale proportionally.

Colours

The three OpenMBEE primary colours, plus one accent for light backgrounds, where the light blue is too pale to read.

/* OpenMBEE workshop colours */
:root {
  --openmbee-dark-blue:  #10052F;
  --openmbee-light-blue: #64DDFA;
  --openmbee-grey:       #D6D6D6;
  --openmbee-accent:     #3E8DB5; /* light backgrounds only */
}

Typography

Use ABeeZee Regular, the OpenMBEE typeface, for workshop headings and body text. The lettering inside the badges is already converted to outlines, so it needs no font.

OPENMBEE
2027 WORKSHOP

at INCOSE IW · at Caltech

Download ABeeZee from Google Fonts (open source, SIL Open Font License)

Usage

Do

  • Use the badge that matches the venue you are promoting: INCOSE IW or Caltech.
  • Use the dark version on dark or photographic backgrounds with enough contrast, and the light version on white or pale backgrounds.
  • Write the name as OpenMBEE Workshop 2027 in running text.

Don’t

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or add shadows or effects.
  • Edit the year or venue text, or merge the two badges.
  • Put the light version on a dark background, or the dark version on a pale one.
  • Use the badge for other events without permission.
