Primary logos

The logo works best against a dark background. This is the primary option.

Download:Stacked SVGHorizontal SVG

Logo usage: using the icon

An icon version can be used in isolation in certain applications, for instance social media or discreet branding where repeating the primary logo would be overkill (such as in the footer of every page in a presentation).

Logo usage: clear zone

To ensure the logo stands out, keep a minimum isolation zone around it.

Clear zone = height of text plus space to icon
